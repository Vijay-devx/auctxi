# How to Participate in an Auction (Clients Only)

As a Client (Team Owner), your goal is to build the best team by winning bids during live auctions.

## Preparation
1. **Wallet**: Ensure your wallet has sufficient funds. You can add funds via the Payments dashboard. (Note: The current system uses a Mock Payment Gateway).
2. **Invitations**: You must be invited to own a team by an Auction Manager. You will see your invitations on your dashboard.
3. **Acceptance**: Accept the invitation to formally link your account to that team for the upcoming auction.

## Live Bidding
- Navigate to the Live Auction room when the auction starts.
- When a player is "On the Block", you can place a bid.
- The system automatically enforces the minimum increment and checks if your team has enough remaining purse limit.
- If you win, the player is added to your roster, and the bid amount is deducted from your purse.
